# Demo talking points (2–3 minutes)

1. **Promise upfront.** "Nothing about your camera leaves this device. MediaPipe runs entirely in-browser." Show the intro.
2. **Solar System.** Point out real orbits — Mars is where it should be for the simulated date. Compare inner planets compressed to visible scale vs true radii. Fly to Earth (clouds, night lights, terminator).
3. **Milky Way.** Show differential rotation; bulge/arms; fly to Sgr A* accretion torus. Warp eases (no hard cut) and audio lifts.
4. **Deep Universe.** Cosmic web filaments, camera-locked nebula dome, CMB/quasar distances (billions of years of light travel). Mention 260–600 Mpc scale.
5. **Constellations.** 9,983 real HYG stars to mag 6.6; 88 IAU figures; Milky Way band; pick a familiar one (UMa/Cas/Orion).
6. **Controls.** Fist brakes, two-hand twist/pan, point+hold selects. Calibration box. Cheat sheet (?) reveals the vocabulary. Falls back to mouse/scroll/two-finger if camera off.
